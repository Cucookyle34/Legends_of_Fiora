The Vetresh Empire was founded around the year 1450 by the first King, [[Conan Vetresh]] on what would later be named [[The Vetresh Peninsula]]. When first created it was a way to unite the [[Human]] race, not out of hate for other races but out of concern for the continuation of humans. Over time, humans advanced their own [[Vetreshian]] technology and deemed other races inferior. Eventually [[Demons]] started to invade places outside of Valla. This trigged the Vetresh Empire to take action starting [[The First Human-Demon War]]. It was during this war when [[Aegis]] proved himself and took over the throne from [[Virion Vetresh]]. 

When Aegis became king he appointed 5 Houses to help him rule. [[The House of Rose]], [[The House of Mogra]], [[The House of Kaner]], [[The House of Iris]], and [[The House of Freesia]]. The Vetresh Empire became a much better place after the first Human-Demon war with King Aegis at the helm. The Empire became more diverse and trade with other countries such as [[Eshos]] and [[The Twin Lake Dynasty]] was booming. Aegis had brought in [[The Era of Peace]]. 

Nearing the year 1650 King Aegis had fallen terribly ill and had become bedridden. He had to relinquish most of his duties to the 5 houses and he refused to see anybody until he got better saying that "A King should look strong for his people" despite his advisors urging him to at least show his face to the public to let them know that everything will be fine. Around the same time life in the empire got worse, discrimination against other races was at an all time high and that lead to more crime. Relations with other nations deteriorated and Demons were back on the offensive. Eventually, it was found that the House of Kaner was responsible for the civil unrest as they were the spreading misinformation about the other races and using that chaos to smuggle illegal goods into their territory. The House of Kaner was dismantled and the rest of the Houses were left to clean up the mess.

The Vetresh Empire is still trying to shape up while the King remains ill. Now entering [[The Second Human-Demon War]] time will tell what this Empire will do.
