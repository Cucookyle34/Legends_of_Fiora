Warlocks are magical beings who gain power through connecting with a [[Local God]]. There are multiple ways people can become Warlocks. One way to become a Warlock is to show potential early on in life and be presented to a Local God who can choose to accept or deny your training to becoming a Warlock. Two examples of Warlocks being chosen this way is [[Caol Wud]], and [[Ove Finnvidsson]]. The other way is if a person reaches out across [[Leylines]] and [[Manaveins]] and pledges allegiance to a Local God. The later is the more common method but also Warlocks who use that method come out not as powerful as being directly trained.

Warlocks complete tasks for their God to obtain more power or gain wisdom. Gods typically ask their Warlocks to do tasks such as maintaining Leylines and Manaveins or deliver messages to authorities and non magical beings.
