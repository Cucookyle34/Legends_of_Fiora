Emre is Edens [[Player Character]] and is a [[Variant Human]] [[Artificer]].

# Background

Born on the continent of [[Kandoka]] in the Slums of [[Argive]], the capital of [[The Vetresh Empire]] to his mother, [[Vera Killic]], and father, [[Damier Killic]], Emre was a very happy young boy and would often be found digging around behind weaponsmiths for scrap metal and old tools to tinker with with his father. At home Emre lived with his parents and his grandmother, [[Hilda Myrsky]] who was very ill. Emre got along well with his grandmother and loved to show her the things he made. Around the age of 5 is when he started to understand that his grandmother needed medicine to live but that his parents couldn't afford it. That was when he decided 2 things, to try and help by fixing things and selling them, and that he was going to get good enough to fix anything, even death itself. Emre got good at tuning weapons and started fixing weapons for soldiers in the slums. One day when fixing a crossbow on the streets Emre was so focused that when he looked up and no one was around he were startled. He looked around but no one was there, suddenly a bag was placed over his head and he blacked out.

The next thing he knew, he was in [[The Experiments]]. Emre was in a dungeon underneath Argive. It was there where he would meet the rest of the party and [[Rith]]. Just like the others Emre was constantly being tested and taken in and out of rooms. One day he was taken to a room and placed upon an altar. Emre was infused with mana through injection points on his left arm. There was so much mana that his body couldn't take it and his arm became brittle and disintegrated off his body. Because of this the researchers gave him a new metal arm.

Some time after getting his metal arm Emre and the others were approached by the guards and sent to fight on the front lines of [[The Second Human-Demon War]].  Emre cant remember much about the war, only that when they had returned 3 years later they were hailed as hero's of the Vetresh Empire for driving the Demons back to their capital of Nyx on [[Valla]].

After returning to Argive, Emre went back to see his family who were both surprised and relived to see him after worrying about him for 6 years. Emre explains briefly to them about how he got his arm and about how he cant remember the war but that now he is closer than every to finding the cure to everything. His mother and father see this look in his eyes and they start to argue about life, death, and who gets to decide what. Emre gets really pissed off at his parents because all he is trying to do is help people. Before leaving home Emre goes into the other room to see his grandmother. He promises her that he will be back, and that next time they see each other he will have found a cure for her.

After leaving home, Emre wanders the world for a while exploring the vast continents of [[Kandoka]] and [[Zhalfir]], looking for any clues about life and longevity. Emre eventually stumbles upon the [[Feywilds]], the land of the [[Lyeiroth Republic]]. It is there where he finds creatures live long lives and illness seems to be non-existent. Emre spends the rest of his days on the outskirts of the Republic watching from afar and honing his skills eventually creating his weapon, [[Farmor]]. It is with this weapon that he earn the name "Hundu" amongst the intelligent fey.
