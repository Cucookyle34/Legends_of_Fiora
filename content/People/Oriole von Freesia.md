Oriole is Andrews [[Player Character]] and is a [[Human]] [[Paladin]].

# Background

Oriole von Freesia sat on the arm of his father’s throne, that is, he had a specially made miniature throne that allowed him to see every decision his father made. His father, [[Leonid von Freesia]], being the head of [[The House of Freesia]], was responsible for managing the agriculture and natural resources of [[The Vetresh Empire]]. Rather than sit around and frolic with other noble children, he would dutifully sit as he watched his father wave off inane requests and childish complaints. Disregarding the fact he was the son of a provincial lord, even he as an eight year old knew how childish these requests were. What mattered to Oriole’s father was that the province stayed stable by all means possible.

And stable it was.

The province of [[Nicolia]] was prosperous, but there was a sense of doom and gloom that Oriole would hear the servants talk about when they thought the young lord was out of earshot. The fear that would strike their voices when talking about the iron fist that Oriole’s father would have when managing matters of state or the iron claw that Oriole’s mother [[Lady Vulpin]] took when ordering around the castle was evident in the way their voices trembled.

“Be strong, use your strength to get what you deserve. What is best for the state is what’s best for you,” his father would say.

“Be smart, use the brain you’ve been given to understand the people around you. Promises are meant to be broken when they no longer serve you,” his mother would say.

Oriole never understood the doom and gloom, not from the moment he was born to when his father appointed him to take on matters of his own when he was fourteen. For three years Oriole would sit through the minor matters of House Freesia, the provincial matters. The matters of whose goat belonged to who or the minor marriage settlements that never truly struck his interest. He wanted his father’s position, to manage over the whole province. He wanted to go beyond what his father failed to achieve with the small moments of kindness he allotted his enemies. That kindness was holding his house back.

Seventeen was how old Oriole was when his house fell. Mutiny was what his parents screamed when being carted away. It wasn’t as if anyone had died under their reign, and if they did then that was a matter of them not being strong enough to manage under a reign that didn’t prioritize the individual person. The people should’ve understood. Even with his parents imprisoned and the people setting up a minor people’s led democracy, Oriole knew that he could bring back the glory of his old house. He knew that he was smart enough and cunning enough to campaign for himself. Not that he had any experience in campaigning given that his family had been in power since he was born, but he could study a thing or two.

### 15 Year Gap
Oriole was taken from his home and separated from the people he once knew. He was carted away to the capital of Argive where he was thrown into a dungeon under the capital. It was there where he stayed for many years getting tortured alone until his mind became numb.

### Present
Apparently, democracy is incredibly popular amongst the people, but very hated by the Empire. Morals do not indicate strength, though, and Oriole could see the cracks where the Empire could fall apart. He knew he could bring back the stability of his house if he was in power. He’d break down weakness. Oriole would become the strength of the nation. Eventually.
