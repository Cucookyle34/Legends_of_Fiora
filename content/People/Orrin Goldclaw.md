Orrin is Megan's [[Player Character]] and is a red [[Dragonborn]] [[Barbarian]].

# Background

Orrin was born in [[Verthicha Shripomn]], a medium sized village in the [[Sarkinian Empire]] on the edge of [[The Still Expanse]] at the base of the [[Skybreen Mountains]]. Orrin was born to his mother [[Okhi Goldclaw]], and father [[Zysan Goldcalw]]. Orrin's mother tragically died at his birth and so Orrin was primarily raised by his father. Orrin was a rambunctious child who liked to run around with friends and explore. One day Orrin's best friend [[Lini]] comes and asks him to explore [[Ravens Run]] with her. Orrin knew that they weren't supposed to venture into Ravens Run without an adult be he figured if he had his fathers magical necklace they would be fine. So the pair took off and explored the passage for the day. They eventually made it to the other side and found a vast snowy plains, they frolicked in the snow for hours as it was something they only ever saw from the base of the mountains. They played until the sun was almost set when they decided it was time to return, they both turn to start back down Ravens Run when Orrin realized that he had dropped his fathers necklace. Orrin couldn't get back without it and so he had turned around to go and look for it. He searches the snow with no luck finding it until he caught a glimmer from the corner of his eye. He goes over and sees his fathers necklace, with a face full of relief he bends down to pick it up a bag gets pulled over his head and he falls unconscious.

Orrin was brought far from his home to [[Argive]], the capital of [[The Vetresh Empire]]. There Orrin stayed in a dungeon underneath the capital undergoing what was called [[The Experiments]]. It was there where Orrin meets the rest of the party as well as [[Rith]]. Orrin watches as people come and go whilst also being moved around himself. Taking tests and undergoing trials. This was until one day when he was taken out of the general holding cell and brought into a different room that he had never been in before. In the room lied a stone alter in the center and on the far wall was what looked to be a [[Giant Warhammer]], Orrin was placed upon the alter and strapped down. The scientists cut open his hand and placed a small green gem on the back of his hand and placed a symbol on his palm. They took him back to the room where he was thrown to the floor. After some time the wound healed and Orrin learned to control what little mana he had to call fourth the Warhammer.

A few weeks later everyone is brought of of the cell and sent to fight on the front lines of [[The Second Human-Demon War]]. Orrin doesn't remember the war only coming back and being hailed a hero of the Vetresh Empire for driving the Demons back to their capital of Nyx on the continent of [[Valla]].

Soon upon arriving back in the capital of Argive Orrin decided to try and stay on the front lines to continue to fight the Demon forces. Orrin asks a commanding officer if he could rejoin the fight. After a while the officer comes back and offers Orrin a job on the front lines with conditions. Orrin was to serve on the front lines indefinitely and would be paid in 2 meals a day and a place to sleep. Orrin agrees and get transported to the front lines.

Orrin served in many battles as well as the occasional guard duty. On one such occasion he saw this sad little 17 year old [[Tiefling]] dragging a Warhammer returning from a battle. Orrin keeps thinking about the boy until his shift is up and goes to the mess hall to eat. You see the Tiefling curled up in the corner with bruises all over, presumably he was beaten by the other soldiers. Orrin goes up to him and try to comfort him but instead kind of scares him. Orrin learns his name is [[Anakis]] and that all he wants to do is restore peace to the world and for people to not hate him. Orrin sees him here and there and gets put on assignments with him for around 2 years. Until one day Orrin wakes up early as Anakis and him are assigned to a mission together that day. Orrin finds that he is later than usual and decides to check his sleeping quarters and finds that he is not there, Orrin checks the mess hall and he his not there either. Just in case Orrin makes a perimeter around the base to make sure he hadn't wandered off and around the corner Orrin sees it, Anakis impaled through the chest by a longsword. Orrin stumbles closer as he sees the body of the boy he watched grow up, lifeless on the ground, as Orrin approaches he sees a note stuck to the sword reading “Dirty Tiefling." That puts Orrin into a silent rage as he finds the people he thinks are responsible for his death and kill them all in their sleep, he decimates the ground beneath him in wild swings of his Warhammer. Orrin realizes that he can't stay so he grabs his belongings and leaves. He works as a mercenary traveling down the continent towards [[Lagos]], the capital of [[Libertia]].
