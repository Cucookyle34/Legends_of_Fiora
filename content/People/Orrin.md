Orrin is Megan's [[Player Character]] and is a red [[Dragonborn]] [[Barbarian]].

# Background

Orrin was born in [[Verthicha Shripomn]], a medium sized village in the [[Sarkinian Empire]] on the edge of [[The Still Expanse]] at the base of the [[Skybreen Mountains]]. Orrin was born to his mother [[Okhi]], and father [[Zysan]]. Orrin's mother tragically died at his birth and so Orrin was primarily raised by his father. Orrin was a rambunctious child who liked to run around with friends and explore. One day Orrin's best friend [[Lini]] comes and asks him to explore [[Ravens Run]] with her. Orrin knew that they weren't supposed to venture into Ravens Run without an adult be he figured if he had his fathers magical necklace they would be fine. So the pair took off and explored the passage for the day. They eventually made it to the other side and found a vast snowy plains, they frolicked in the snow for hours as it was something they only ever saw from the base of the mountains. They played until the sun was almost set when they decided it was time to return, they both turn to start back down Ravens Run when Orrin realized that he had dropped his fathers necklace. Orrin couldn't get back without it and so he had turned around to go and look for it. He searches the snow with no luck finding it until he caught a glimmer from the corner of his eye. He goes over and sees his fathers necklace, with a face full of relief he bends down to pick it up a bag gets pulled over his head and he falls unconscious.

Orrin was brought far from his home to [[Argive]], the capital of [[The Vetresh Empire]]. There Orrin stayed in a dungeon underneath the capital undergoing what was called [[The Experiments]]. It was there where Orrin meets the rest of the party as well as [[Rith]].
