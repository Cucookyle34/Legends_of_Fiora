Orrin is Megan's [[Player Character]] and is a red [[Dragonborn]] [[Barbarian]].

Orrin was born in [[Verthicha Shripomn]], a medium sized village in the [[Sarkinian Empire]] at the base of the [[Skybreen Mountains]]. Orrin was born to his mother [[Okhi]], and father [[Zysan]]. Orrin's mother tragically died at his birth and so Orrin was primarily raised by his father. Orrin was a rambunctious child who liked to run around with friends and explore. One day Orrin's best friend [[Lini]] comes and asks him to explore [[Ravens Run]] with her. Orrin knew that they weren't supposed to venture into Ravens Run without an adult be he figured if he had the magical necklace they would be fine.
