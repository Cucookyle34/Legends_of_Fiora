[[Halfling]]

Tolman is the father of [[Caol Wud]] and a swordsman that helps to protect [[The Firo Territories]]. He settled down in [[Lumas Village]] with his wife [[Nora Wud]] after traveling as an adventurer between the continents of [[Littjara]] and [[Kandoka]].
