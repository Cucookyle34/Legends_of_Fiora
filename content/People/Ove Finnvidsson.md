Ove is Ava's [[Player Character]] and is a [[Panserbjorn]] [[Warlock]].

# Background

Ove was born to his mother, [[Sigrid Finnvidsson]] and father, [[Rakni Finnvidsson]] who lived alone together in unclaimed territory on the tundra of [[Zhalfir]] known as the [[Newfell Ice Plains]]. They raised him till he was 4 years old when he started to show signs of becoming a powerful magic user. They brought him to the [[Local God]] [[Neva]] to be trained as a Warlock. Ove doesn't remember his parents very well, only that they brought him to Neva. Ove trained under Neva for a long time, doing menial tasks such as cleaning and delivering messages but also training to become a fully fledged warlock under Neva. Ove would meditate and work on controlling the flow of mana through [[Manaveins]] and [[Leylines]]. This would continue until he was 7 when Neva gave him the task to meditate under a manavein a days journey from the temple as it was knotted up and needed fixing. This was nothing Ove hadn't done before and so he set out, eager to complete his task. On the walk over he saw a small platoon of human soldiers heading towards the temple, he was nervous at first but knew he had to follow through with Neva's wishes. Upon reaching the spot Ove begins to meditate and untangle the manavein. A few hours pass and he is almost finished with the knot when he feels a bag quickly drawn over his face as he blacks out.

He wakes up on the floor of a dungeon underground. That's where he meets the rest of the party and [[Rith]]. Over time he learns that he was taken far away from his home and to [[Argive]], the capital of [[The Vetresh Empire]] and that he is part of what's being called [[The Experiments]]. Ove watches many people come and go as he is taken in and out of rooms for testing. One day he is brought into a room with a stone altar and shelves on the walls lined with jars of eyes. He is placed onto the altar and chained down, the experimenters remove your left eye and replace it with another one from the shelf, it doesn't work so they do it again and again until they find one that works. They have given him a [[Demon Eye]].

After receiving the eye it takes him a few weeks to get used to it, the eye grants him the ability to look into the future but without controlling the mana going into it, all he can see is variations of the future all at once. After a while he can control the eye but still gets headaches from it every once in a while. More time passes and eventually everyone is taken out of the cell and gets sent to the front lines of [[The Second Human-Demon War]].

Ove doesn't remember the war that well only that it was unpleasant. The main thing that Ove remembered was returning back to Argive after 3 years to be hailed a hero of the Vetresh Empire for driving the Demons back to their capital of Nyx on [[Valla]].

When the Vetresh Empire had enough of Ove, they brought him along with [[Caol Wud]] to the [[Brishall Marsh]]. There they were abandoned. After a few hours of wandering the marsh they were spotted and approached by [[Arti]]. Arti introduces himself and takes the two in as part of his crew of pirates. From there they sail the northern waters carrying cargo and occasionally plundering a ship or two. After reaching a port on the northern shore of [[The Still Expanse]] in the [[Sarkinian Empire]], Ove leaves Caol, Arti, and the crew after spotting the snowy [[Skybreen Mountains]] in the distance. Arti is sad to see Ove go and leaves him with a [[Feather Necklace]] made from his own feathers. From there they part ways and Ove spends some time in the port town gathering information on the world and learning Draconic.

After taking a while to prepare for the trip into the mountains Ove sets off in search of Neva. A few days into the journey Ove is walking down in one of the mountain valleys when suddenly a large yellow scaled [[Dragon]] swoops down and lands in front of him. The dragon tries to intimidate him by towering over him and staring him down. Ove understands that the dragon is just protecting its territory and so Ove shows his intentions by slowly approaching the dragon and giving the mightiest bear hug to its leg. It understands that you mean no harm and it walks away, you decide to follow it all the way up to its cave high in the mountains. Its there where you find its hatchlings, they take affection to you and you decide to stay for a while as you explore the mountains in search for Neva.
