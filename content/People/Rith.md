Rith is a red female [[Dragonborn]] [[NPC]]
She is from a city in [[The Still Expanse]] with her twin brother [[Arashin]]

What the party doesn't know and what Rith had never told them is that she and Arashin were born with a special magic that allows them to communicate over long distances almost instantly using [[Leylines]] and [[Manaveins]].

Usually they would be very carful to hide this from people as it is a very rare magic that very little people ever get the chance to encounter let alone understand. But on one occasion they were caught and got turned in by a previous party member that had betrayed them in hopes that they would get a huge payout by [[The Vetresh Empire]].

Rith was in [[The Experiments]] 3 years before the rest of the party arrived. When more children would arrive she would always be the first to run and comfort them. Throughout the Experiments she is unable to contact Arashin.

Eventually she gets sent to fight in [[The Second Human-Demon War]] along with the other [[Player Character]]s and people from the Experiments. During a battle on the shores of Valla she realizes that some of the [[Demons]] aren't trying to kill the children but capture them. She decides to save the others by covering their escape back to the boat but gets captured herself in the process.

She winds up in a dungeon underneath the Demon capital city of [[Nyx]]. She is repeatedly tortured to try and break her mental to create an opening of the soul of [[Pride]] to take over her body. She never gives in and eventually after a year or so the Demons give up and kill her. They then resurrect her as an undead version of herself fully loyal to the Demons.
