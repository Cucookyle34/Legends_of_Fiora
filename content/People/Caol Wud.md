Caol is Joes [[Player Character]] and is a [[Halfling]] [[Warlock]].

# Background

Caol was born on the northern side of the continent of [[Kandoka]] in a place called [[Lumas Village]] in the [[Fields of Firo]], close to the [[Glastion Peninsula]]. Caol was raised by his parents [[Tolman Wud]] and [[Nora Wud]] until the age of 2 when Caol showed that he had an affinity for becoming a Warlock. He was then brought to the [[Local God]] [[Glasto]] to be trained. For 11 years Caol trained under Glasto, learning all a Warlock needs to know. On Caols 13th birthday he walks to the temple under orders from Glasto. It was then that Caol was kidnapped by a secret organization under the control of [[The Vetresh Empire]].

Caol was brought far away from his home to [[Argive]], the capital of The Vetresh Empire. It was there where he was brought into a dungeon far underground to [[The Experiments]]. It was there where he met the rest of the party as well as [[Rith]]. During the Experiments he was constantly being tested and measured. Then one day Caol was brought into a room and placed on a stone altar. It was on this altar that he was infused with so much mana that his [[Gate]] expanded and allowed him to obtain much more power through the [[Leylines]] and [[Manaveins]]. 

After a few years in the Experiments the guards take Caol and the rest of the children left and send him to the front lines of the [[The Second Human-Demon War]]. He doesn't remember much about the war only returning 3 years later being hailed as a hero by the Vetresh Empire for driving the Demons back to their capital of Nyx on the continent of [[Valla]].

When the Vetresh Empire had enough of Caol, they brought him along with [[Ove Finnvidsson]] to the [[Brishall Marsh]]. There they were abandoned. After a few hours of wandering the marsh they were spotted and approached by [[Arti]]. Arti introduces himself and takes the two in as part of his crew of pirates. From there they sail the northern waters carrying cargo and occasionally plundering a ship or two. Ove leaves the crew after they stopped at a port on the coast of the Still Expanse. Caol continues on with Arti exploring the northern lands until one day Arti makes one of his usual not so smart moves and accidently gets the ship caught in a storm and violently thrown off course. The ship enters what's known as [[The Blood Sea]], with conditions worsening the ship gets thrown around on the waves and out of the waters rises a 40ft tall crustation like appendage. It falls down upon the ship splitting it in half and throwing Caol, Arti, and the rest of the crew into the waters.

Caol wakes up on a beach, presumably an Island to the north of [[Littjara]], no one else in sight. He wanders the Island for a bit and discovers what looks like the remnants of an old path, he follows it up to the center of the Island and finds a large area with a [[Strange Stone]] in the middle of it. He walks up to the stone and touches it. In that instant a flash of light consumes his view and as it fades he sees an entirely different environment. Using his background as an explorer he was able to deduce his location to be in a small forest on the [[Kessig Plains]] just south of [[Lethe]] in the country of [[Eshos]]. He decides its probably best to head to the capital, towards civilization.
