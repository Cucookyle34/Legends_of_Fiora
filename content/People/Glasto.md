Glasto is the [[Local God]] created by [[Hydaelyn]] that resides on the [[Glastion Peninsula]]. Glasto prefers that his followers train in their own and see him every so often. He doesn't dislike the mortal races but prefers to keep them out of sight when possible. He thinks himself above the mortals and doesn't understand their struggles. Because of this location in the Jungle most of the abilities he offers his followers are themed around the jungle.
