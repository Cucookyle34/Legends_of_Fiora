It was said that the people of the province of [[Nicolia]] rose up in anger that their lord was not treating them right. It was said that this was all the doing of a local farmer who orchestrated the takedown of [[The House of Freesia]]. That it was the will of the people who took down [[Leonid von Freesia]] and his family. The reality is much more grim. in truth it was [[Lucian Valeria Rose]], the head of [[The House of Rose]] who used the people of the realm to take down Leonid, as he was coming too close to uncovering the truth behind [[The House of Rose]].
