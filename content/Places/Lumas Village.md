Lumas Village is a medium sized Village in the [[Fields of Firo]] just south of the [[Glastion Peninsula]]. It being so close to the Jungle means that the people in this village rely on resources from the Jungle as their main source of income (Herbs and food that can only grow in a Jungle environment). Dangerous monsters from the Jungle are more likely to attack the village due to proximity so the village is in constant need of protection. They get protection from [[The Adventurers Guild]] in town.
