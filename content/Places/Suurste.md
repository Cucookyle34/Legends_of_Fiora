The old capital of [[The Vetresh Empire]] under the [[Vetresh Family]]. Now it has become a major trading city and home to the largest port on [[Fiora]]. It sits on the coast of [[The Great Aerie]] across from the [[The Vetresh Peninsula]]. After the capital changed to [[Argive]] it became more diverse but a lot of the older citizens still have prejudice against the other races. You can find most of the common [[Guilds]] here such as [[The Adventurers Guild]], [[The Merchants Guild]], and [[The Artisans Guild]]. Many people departing from The Vetresh Peninsula heading east stop at Suurste to being their journey.
