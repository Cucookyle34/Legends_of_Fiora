Kandoka is the second largest continent on [[Fiora]]. It is home to many species and has many defining features. 

Its northern half consists of the [[Glastion Peninsula]], [[Fields of Firo]], and the [[Eloren Wilds]]. This northern land is a very diverse region with many different species living together in small scattered villages. The Fields of Firo is a place that many [[Halfling]] call home, while one of the [[Local God]]s, [[Garruk]] resides in the Eloren Wilds.

The southern half consists of [[The Great Aerie]], [[The Vetresh Peninsula]], and [[The Krosan Cliffs]]. The Vetresh Peninsula is home to [[The Vetresh Empire]] and its capital of [[Argive]], a primarily human empire. The Great Aerie is a vast plains that spans most of the continent and similar to the Fields of Firo, it is more diverse featuring scattered villages. The Krosan Cliffs are known for their towering stance overlooking the [[Kessig-Vetresh Ocean]].
