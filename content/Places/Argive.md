Argive is the capital of [[The Vetresh Empire]] located on the northernmost point of [[The Vetresh Peninsula]]. The capital under the [[Vetresh Family]] was [[Suurste]] but moved once [[Aegis]] became King. The capital has everything anyone could ever want, and most [[Guilds]] have an HQ in Argive. Argive is very diverse and many different kinds of people live or gather there.
